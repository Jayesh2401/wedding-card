## Carousel

The wedding carousel has ten ordered photos: `carousel-photo-01.png` through `carousel-photo-10.png`. Keep the two-digit numbering to preserve the slide order. When replacing a carousel photo, also update its `alt` text in `index.html` if the subject changes.
